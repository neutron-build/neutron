(b) *The waits for a load*: a load takes only its MapLeaf's latch shared (a MapLeaf's load its MapInner's), whose holders obtain no frame by eviction (§4.1: copies come from the reserve, MapLeafs are pinned before any map latch) and, by the new rule and MAPLEAF-WAIT-UNDER-INNER's, wait for no load; its frames come under §5.4's count, which never waits on an unpin; and a load whose thread restarts or waits for the flip ends first. So a waiter of a load never waits on a thread that waits on it, and the two new hold components of the fairness bound are each bounded. (c) *Reload only after the flip*: adds a wait on the flip by a thread holding no latch, no gate and no page being loaded, which §9.2 item 3 now names among the gate waiters, so the poison wakes it. (e) *FREED-FRAME-WRITTEN's step-2 half*: §13 step 2 already selects dirty frames only. None of (a) to (e) loses an acknowledged commit silently (each failure ends in the poison and recovery replays the commit, §9.2 item 6), adds a cycle, or refuses a state a correct 7.21 build reaches.
