## Findings

### R1 (MEDIUM, must fix): boot-id freshness has no floor from the opened meta's `boot_id`, so after two rollbacks boot ids go backwards and an MP-only history loses acked commits silently

**The applied rule.** §14 step 8 makes a boot's id the newer of two values:

- `low32(ckpt_id)`;
- one above the newest boot id of a record the walk **accepted**.

The draft-2 gate's "the walk's first record never fires falsely" assumed that every later boot's id is newer than the meta it opened. Under this rule that no longer holds, because a raised id can exceed the next boot's `ckpt_id`.

**The trace.** Pure class MP: three LOST meta writes and ordinary crashes, with no WAL fault.

1. **Boot `b−1`** writes meta `k` to slot B and crashes.
2. **Boot `b`** recovers from `k`.
   - It gets `ckpt_id` k+1, so its id is k+1.
   - It writes meta k+1 to slot A, then appends and acks.
   - Meta k+1's write is LOST, then a crash.
3. **Boot `b+1`** recovers from `k`.
   - Its walk crosses `b`'s records, so its id is max(k+1, k+2) = **k+2**.
   - Its meta k+1 (slot A) is LOST again, then a crash.
4. **Boot `b+2`** recovers from `k`.
   - Its walk crosses `b` and `b+1`, so its id is **k+3**.
   - Meta k+1 (slot A, `boot_id` k+3) is durable. Its `wal_start_lsn` is the append position `p`, by §12.6 and step 8's unmarked-chunks sentence.
   - It crashes before any record of its own reads back (no append, or its first record torn).
5. **Boot `b+3`** recovers from meta k+1.
   - Its walk from `p` accepts nothing, so its id is max(low32(k+2), none) = **k+2**.
   - That id is **older than the meta it opened** (k+3), and it **equals `b+1`'s id**.
   - It writes meta k+2 to slot B with `boot_id` k+2, appends and acks `on` commits from `p` with boot id k+2, and crashes before its next checkpoint.
6. **A LOST write on meta k+2** leaves slot B holding meta `k`.
7. **The next open** selects meta k+1 (`boot_id` k+3) and walks from `p`.
   - The first record is `b+3`'s, with id k+2. That is older than the meta's `boot_id`, so the walk **stops at `p`**.
   - The valid end `p` equals `wal_ckpt_end`.
   - `ts_ckpt_hi` is the boot's start frontier, so the `ts_ckpt_hi` condition holds.
   - So I-WAL-COVER passes, and the open returns **`Ok` without `b+3`'s acked commits**.

**What it breaks.** This is the exact loss that §12.5's "A walk may cross into newer boots" exists to prevent: I-REDO-COMPLETE under a meta-slot fault. The rule that edit 1 added causes it. Under BOOT-ID-CKPT-ONLY, ids are monotone and this shape cannot occur, although G1 (i) occurs instead.

**Not caught.** No named test catches it. `t_boot_id_after_rollback` and `t_meta_rollback_crosses_boot` each stop after the first rollback chain.

**The fix.** Add the opened meta's `boot_id` as a third floor. A boot's id is then newer than every surviving meta on its chain, and newer than every accepted record. That restores "boot ids increase across the boots whose records one walk can reach", and the Residual stays exactly as stated.

**Required text.**

1. **S-STORE §14 step 8.** Replace the edit-1 sentence with:

   > "Its boot id is the newest, in serial-number order (§12.5), of the low 32 bits of its `ckpt_id`, one above the opened meta's `boot_id`, and one above the newest boot id of any record the recovery walk accepted. After a meta rollback (§2.2, X5-R5) the walk can cross into a boot whose meta was lost, and whose `ckpt_id` this boot checkpoint would otherwise repeat. A boot whose id was raised that way can be followed by one whose `ckpt_id` is below that id, which the opened meta's `boot_id` bounds. Meta `k+1`'s `boot_id` records it."

2. **New mutant and test variant.**
   - Mutant **BOOT-ID-NO-META-FLOOR**: the opened meta's term is omitted.
   - Variant of `t_boot_id_after_rollback` (A-7d, MP), "raised id, then no records": steps 1–7 above. The open must replay the acked commits that the boot after the raised one wrote. BOOT-ID-NO-META-FLOOR returns `Ok` without them.
   - Add both to the I-WAL-STALE row's mutants and tests.
3. **Mirror the rule elsewhere.**
   - The S-STORE Draft 5 changelog's boot-id freshness line.
   - C-T0 §2.5's definition clause (R2) and the C-T0 8.3 changelog.
   - Card W6 (vi), the Mutation evidence list, and the inheritance rows for A-7c, A-7d and B-1d.

All of this lies within the existing Limits: the §14 step 8 sentence, its mutant and test, the C-T0 §2.5 definition clause, and the changelogs.
