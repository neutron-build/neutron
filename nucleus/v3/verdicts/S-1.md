## Summary

This is a strong draft. Its structure, scope and boundaries are right, and almost every claim checked against arm (b), E0, e1-bench and the decision block holds.

It is not mergeable yet, because one normative recovery rule (§12.6) is narrower than the plan rule it says it restates. Implemented as written, that rule loses acked chunked commits. The spec declares that it wins over the plan, so this cannot merge with a follow-up. Four further fixes are needed: the B-3 space metric, I-SPACE versus pack skips, the G-RESURRECT meaning, and the bulk-load watermark. Each is a small edit, and together they make one rework pass.

## Required fixes (REWORK)

**R-1. §12.6 replay start drops chunks of a marked, unapplied txn (L622-627; I-REDO-COMPLETE L711; mutant L749). Data loss.**
- **The text.** The second term counts only "every transaction that has written chunks but no marker at the flip".
- **Counterexample.** Take a chunked txn `c`:
  - its chunks are at LSN 100-200;
  - a small commit with ts ≤ T is at LSN 300;
  - `c`'s CommitMarker, with `commit_ts = c > T`, was appended at LSN 400 before the flip;
  - `c`'s apply had not finished, so T < c.
- **What goes wrong.**
  - `wal_start_lsn = 400`, because `c` has a marker and so is not counted by the second term.
  - Pass 1 finds the marker, but pass 2 starts at 400 and never sees the chunks.
  - Step 6 may recycle the segment that holds them.
  - `c` is acked (sync-on: after the group sync) yet lost, or left partially applied in the image with nothing to complete it. That breaks I-REDO-COMPLETE and I-CKPT-AT-T (b).
- **The plan's rule covers this case.** PLAN §3.3 says "segments holding chunks of a txn whose marker isn't yet below T are never recycled". L627's claim that the spec "is PLAN-v3's chunk rule, stated as a replay-start position" is therefore false.
- **Fix.**
  - The second term becomes: every txn with chunks whose marker is absent at the flip **or has `commit_ts > T`**.
  - Widen START-LSN-PAST-CHUNK, or add a second mutant, to cover a marker that is > T but sits after the first > T commit's LSN. The current mutant text, "ignores an open transaction's first chunk", misses it.

**R-2. §11 / §19 T2: "tree bytes" is undefined (L561-566, L832), and the obvious reading can never pass.**
- **Where the number comes from.** e1-bench's `tree_bytes` is `st.pages.page_count() * page_size` (arm-b engine.rs `stats`): pages allocated, never freed.
- **Why the obvious reading fails.** Under this spec the file never shrinks (§4.5), so any file-length or `slot_count` reading of "tree bytes" stays at the post-W3 peak. That peak is about 11 versions per key, about 10× the pre-update tree. B-3 would then fail and the T2 trigger would re-open N3 spuriously.
- **Fix.** Pin the metric to the tree's reachable pages: the mapped LPIDs reachable from its root, including overflow chains, × `page_size` (amcheck can count them).
- **Plan issue.** The decision block's "after-GC tree bytes" (PLAN §4, Known costs, "Space amplification") needs the same definition through the §18 plan-sync card.

**R-3. I-SPACE is stated unconditionally, but §6.5 permits skips that break it (L304 vs L556).**
- **The conflict.** A redistribute whose new separator would overflow `P` is skipped, so an underfull leaf can survive a complete sweep, and the "every leaf … `used > F_PACK·U − C_max`" clause is false. A test pinned to that clause would be either flaky or weakened.
- **Fix.** Either:
  - bound the skips, for example by falling back to a merge-only attempt, or by counting skipped steps and excluding those leaves from the clause and the derived bound; or
  - prove that a skip cannot occur under the §3.2 4-cell sizing, if that holds for separators.
- **Also fix step 1 of the sweep (L528).** Step 1 latches only the leaf, while step 4 needs `P` latched before `L` and `R` (I-LATCH, §6.5 preconditions). Step 1 must re-descend latch-coupled and keep the parent, or step 4 must re-descend. As written, step 4 latches upward.

**R-4. G-RESURRECT changes meaning, and the spike's delete order is an unlisted departure (L498, L719, L737).**
- **X-2's mutant.** `verdicts/X-2.md` defines G-RESURRECT as "an elided tombstone at <= W lets the next older version become the kept one", a decision-state bug caught by S13.
- **The spec's mutant.** §10.2 redefines it as an ordering bug ("the tombstone is deleted while an older version remains"). Meanwhile L719 says X-2's mutant names "keep their meaning there".
- **The spike departs too, unlisted.** Arm (b)'s `gc` pushes the tombstone candidate first and deletes the candidates in pass-2 order, i.e. the tombstone **before** its older versions (engine.rs:585-588 pushes it, then the delete loop at engine.rs:599 runs in that order). So the spike transiently violates I-GC-ORDER. The spec should list this as a departure next to the `above_w` one.
- **Fix.** Keep G-RESURRECT with X-2's meaning, and add an ordering mutant (e.g. G-TOMB-FIRST) for I-GC-ORDER.

**R-5. §10.1 bulk-load watermark is off by one (L486).**
- "S-3 registers the load's ts, so `W` cannot pass it" only gives `W ≤ S`.
- A GC with `w = S` then runs while the load's inserts at ts `S` are unfinished, which violates condition 2 as written.
- **Fix.** Require `W < S`, for example by registering `S−1` or by stating a strict inequality.

## Review points (1)-(6)

**(1) Completeness.**
- All seven §3.3 invariants are present: REPLAY-IDEM is merged with ENTRY-UNIQUE.
- Every §9 S-1 deliverable is covered.
- The table has 29 rows, each with a pinning card and a named mutant, and is testable apart from R-3.
- Gaps: N-4 and N-5.

**(2) R2, R3 and R7 are sound and correctly scoped as refinements.**
- **R2.** Applies finish out of ts order (commit.rs: `apply(ts)`, then `mark_applied` in ts order), and §12.4 says apply follows the append. So syncing every record appended before the flip is both necessary and sufficient.
- **R3.** "Pause apply" does not cover GC, eviction or pack SMOs. The try-gate rule for evictors avoids the writer-preference deadlock.
- **R7.** Needed for the E4 ts-prefix oracle.

**(3) Pack, free set and the 1.2× bound.**
- The arithmetic holds: U ≈ 8150 and C_max ≈ 135 (8 + ~5 escape + 10 + 101 + overhead) give 1/(0.85 − 0.0166) ≈ 1.20 ≤ 1.5. Pre-update ≥ cells/U, so the bound holds for any pre-update fill.
- It is valid only with R-2's metric and R-3's skip fix.

**(4) Consistency with arm (b).**
- The encoding matches `encode.rs` byte for byte: `prefix_of`, `encode_key`, `encode_seek_newest` (= `Ts::MAX`), `encode_key_upper` (`0xFF × 9`), `decode_key` from the right, and tags 0/1.
- The scan bounds match `range(prefix_of(lo), prefix_of(hi))`.
- The one-seek read matches `version_at`; the newest probe matches `newest_version`.
- Mismatches: R-4 and N-6.

**(5) Departures.**
- **Justified:** stable root, the meta fields, forced checkpoint for bulk load (with R-5, N-1 and N-2), and reserved tags 6-255 plus ts `u64::MAX` (XX001 until S-2 assigns them).
- **Boundaries clean:** S-2 owns W and floors it at ≥ `gc_w`; R-1 owns `F` and `R_EPOCH`; S-3 owns index-build restart.

**(6) X-2-F1 note: confirmed.**
- **X-2-F1's test ("no readers"):** W = visible_ts = the newer put's ts. The newest ≤ W is then the live put, so the tombstone is an ordinary older version, both rules delete it, and GC-KEEPS-TOMB survives.
- **The spec's test:** a reader registered after the delete pins W at the tombstone's ts. The newer put sits above W, so the spike's `above_w == 0` keeps the tombstone and the X-2-F1 rule elides it. The test catches the mutant.
