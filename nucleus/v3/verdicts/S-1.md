## Rulings on the two departures

1. **No R-5 mutant: accepted.**
   - The load writes only live entries, all at S, into an unpublished tree.
   - A GC with `w = S` racing those inserts finds each loaded entry either behind its position, where the entry is newer than everything it kept, or ahead of it, where the entry is the newest <= w.
   - The worst outcome is under-pruning. No read at any `S' >= W` changes, so a mutant (register S, not S−1) is unobservable without a new hook. Condition 2 is a sound formal precondition. S-3 should record this reasoning.
2. **Count-and-exclude over prove-zero-skips: accepted.**
   - Zero skips cannot be proved. Separators may include ts bytes (§6.4), so a redistribute can lengthen a separator in a nearly full parent.
   - Counting keeps I-SPACE testable and keeps B-3's bound honest.
   - B-3 reports `skips` beside the measurement, and the 1.5× acceptance is still measured directly.

## Remaining defects -> S-1-F1 (P, Class: spec; must merge before A-4b's I-SPACE test)

1. **The sweep cascade can falsify I-SPACE (§10.4 step 5, §6.5, §11).**
   - **(a) Left-neighbour cascade.** Take a rightmost-child inner page P with `used < U/4`. By §6.5 ("the same way") it packs with its **left** neighbour P_l. P_l's former rightmost child, which was exempt and possibly underfull, then becomes a middle child beside P's first child. The sweep has already passed both and never packs them together, so the I-SPACE leaf clause is false. A redistribute (rotation) into P_l does the same.
   - **(b) The cascade does not recurse.** It checks only L's parent P. A grandparent that loses a child in P's pack is never checked, so the inner clause is false for trees of height >= 4. A-4b's small-page proptests can reach that height.
   - **Fix.** Either:
     - in the sweep, pack an inner page only with its right neighbour (rightmost children are already exempt), and repeat the check one level up whenever the page whose children just finished is its parent's rightmost child; or
     - list the junction leaf in I-SPACE's exceptions and state the recursion.

   The derived B-3 bound is unaffected: at most about one extra page per inner page, under 1%.
2. **§10.1's S−1 pin must come before S is chosen.** W is monotonic (§1), and `W = visible_ts = S` when no older snapshot is registered, so a registration at S−1 made after taking S cannot hold W down. The text should say that S-3 registers first and then picks S above the pin, for example by registering R and loading at `S = R+1` once `visible_ts > R`. It should also record ruling 1's reasoning.
3. **§13's FreeSet convergence sentence.** It must also count the part `r` of the original allocation that lands in the new slots: the requirement is `r + extra <= g`. This holds, since the FreeSet is a handful of pages when `g` is small, but the sentence states only `extra < g`.
4. **Nit, §6.5 redistribute for inner pages.** The cell that enters L is P's separator, not R's first cell. The "fits" test and the Full claim should say so.
