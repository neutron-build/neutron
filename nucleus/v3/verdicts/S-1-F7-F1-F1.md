## Can a split still free a chain?

**Not through the prune, at any call site.** §10.3's Chains bullet forbids deleting any tag-4, tag-5 or tag-8 entry, dead or not, and states the rule for every call site (before a split, any exclusively held leaf, eviction nested under another hold, §5.2). The two delete routes inside the prune are both closed: the "newer `≤ w` precedes it" delete is restricted to tags 0–3 and dead 6–7, and the elide now requires that no older version of the group holds a chain. The elide's "on the leaf covers the whole group" argument is right: the tombstone is newest `≤ w`, versions sort ts-descending (§7.2, I-ENC-ORDER), and the different-key test proves the group ends on this leaf, so every older version is visible to the chain check. A kept tombstone with its non-chain older versions deleted is read-equivalent (the tombstone shadows them, I-GC-READEQ), and I-GC-ORDER holds: the tombstone stays until the sweep has deleted the chain-bearing versions first. The prune never packs (§6.5), so it frees no LPID either, and the claim that it "needs no MapLeaf pins" holds.

**Through a writer's own replace, yes; pre-existing and outside this card's Limits (follow-up S-1-F7-F1-F1-F1).** §6.7 "Freeing" and C-T0 v8 §2.4 free a chain in the gated operation that replaces its entry: an owner's provisional rewrite of a tag-8 entry, a settle (b) or (c) by a writer on `k` that deletes an aborted or dead tag-8 entry, and a superseding replay op (§14 step 4). The insert that does this descends with exclusive coupling. If its leaf is not safe (§6.2), as on any leaf where the new value is larger or the leaf is near full, it still holds `P`, and if it splits, the free comes after the split's mutations. The chain's pages are then read for their next pointers under `P`'s exclusive latch after the first mutation, which is the §5.3/§5.6 conflict this card removed from the prune. No text says the replaced chain is freed holding only the leaf. This card's text does not claim otherwise (§6.4 says only that the prune reads no page; the status line says the prune frees no chain), so it is not a defect here.
