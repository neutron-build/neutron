**R2 (answered; the mutant is real but conditional).** The new sentence gives a pre-validation mismatch an outcome: retry if the entry changed, XX001 only under an unchanged entry. That is right: under an unchanged entry Work 3's exactness argument says the LPID is the tree's root for the hold, and CreateTree installs the entry only after the root is initialised under its latch, so a mismatch there is corruption. On the question put to this gate: ROOT-HEADER-MISMATCH-FATAL is an honest equivalent-where mutant, not one that can never fail. On a path that latches first the window does not exist, no mismatch arises except corruption, and the mutant is correct behaviour. The outcome is the same (no XX001, no property check), and the sentence becomes literally true under -F4b's blocking latch. All three go to F1; none blocks.
