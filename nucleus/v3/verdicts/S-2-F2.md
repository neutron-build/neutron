## R6: met

- **Durability, not return.** §12.5 now says a writer appends to a segment only after its creation or recycling rename (§12.1) and its preallocated length are **durable**, "not merely returned". It names the mechanism: the segment `sync_all`'d and its `wal/` directory entry synced through `Env`, or the platform's equivalent. It then says "no record in it is acked before then".
  - This is stronger than round 2 asked ("acks no record"). It forbids the append too, which is sound and costs nothing at steady state, because segments are prepared ahead.
  - A recycling rename stays inside `wal/`, so the one directory sync covers both the old and the new name.
- **Round 2's failure is closed.** That failure was: an unsynced create, rename or `set_len` is lost after acks, and recovery stops cleanly short of acked commits. A missing or short segment can now hold only unacked records, so "nothing acked lies past that point" holds, and I-REDO-COMPLETE is restored. A power cut before durability loses only unacked appends.
- **The mutant.** SEG-ACK-BEFORE-DURABLE is defined in §12.5 as one falsifiable deviation: append, `sync_data` and ack before the directory entry and length are durable.
- **Its placement.** It sits in the I-RO-OPEN row's mutant column, and the row's test column now reads `A-7d, A-6b`.
- **The catching test.** `t_seg_ack_durable` (A-6b) is named, and both of round 2's cases are covered: lost-create and lost-rename. Power is cut at every step from the create or rename to the ack, and every acked record must replay. Under the mutant, a cut after the ack and before the directory sync loses the segment, so the test fails. It is caught.
- **Its SimEnv dependency is stated.** It needs "a SimEnv whose power cut also loses unsynced creates, renames and `set_len`". Today's SimEnv loses only unsynced writes, and PLAN-v3 §3.2's `Env` has no directory sync, so the test cannot be written until S-2-F2-F1 lands. That is why the verdict is MERGE-WITH-FOLLOWUPS rather than MERGE: the spec is complete, but the test depends on harness work.
- **Changelog.** It says "(R4, R6)" and records the durability premise, the mutant, the test and its cases, and both nits.
