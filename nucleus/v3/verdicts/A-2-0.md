## The implementer's ESCALATE (point 4): ruling

**Where the readings differ.** The card counts page 0 or a copy only when it fully decodes. §2.2's identity bullet counts "page 0's magic and CRC" and "every copy with valid magic and CRC", and its misplaced sentence says "a copy with valid magic and CRC in a place of the other parity". The two differ only on an intact record (magic and CRC valid) with an out-of-domain field. The gate's probes found these shapes (card's reading, then the spec's literal reading):

| Probe | Card's reading | Spec's literal reading |
|---|---|---|
| P4a: page 0 intact, `format_version` 2 / `page_size` 12288 / `checksum_alg` 2 / `wal_seg_size` 0 | opens 10 | XX001 |
| P4b: page 0 torn, the only replica copy intact with `copy` 7 | XX001 | opens 10 (the implementer's case 1) |
| P4c and P4d: page 0 torn, an intact copy with `format_version` 2 beside good ones | opens | XX001 |
| P4e and P4f: an intact misplaced copy 12, malformed (`map_level` 0 or `copy` 7) | opens 10 | XX001 |
| P4i: page 0 intact but malformed, all four places unreadable | XX001 | 58030 |

No correct writer, crash or media fault in §2.2's model produces such a record; a torn record is intact only by a CRC collision, about 2^-32. So the readings agree on everything I-META-COPIES covers.

**The safe long-term design** is neither text as written:

- The card's reading treats an intact record as torn. A creator's bug in page 0 (never rewritten) is then masked for the file's life (P4a opens). In a mixed-version file, a page 0 that a newer format wrote is bypassed, and the copies this build can read open: a silent rollback.
- The spec's literal reading fails closed there (P4a, P4c–P4f). But under both readings, an intact copy with the file's `creation_uuid` and another `format_version` is classed as foreign or invalid and ignored. A file a newer build has written to (a downgrade) then opens its older metas.
- The safe rule: magic and CRC decide what exists; the field domains decide only what may be opened. An intact record that carries the file's `creation_uuid` (or, with page 0 invalid, any intact copy) but is not well-formed fails the open with XX001, naming the place and the field. That covers a future format version, a writer's bug and a deliberate write alike. Salvage loses nothing: it reads with its own lenient decoder and reports the refused record. Up to a CRC collision, no fault the open must survive is refused by it.

**Is the code's behaviour acceptable to merge as it is?** Yes, once RW-1 to RW-3 are in. The difference cannot come from a fault, `select_meta` has no caller until A-7d, and the card pinned this reading openly. It must be settled, spec first, before A-7d calls `select_meta` from an open: A-2-0-F1. The text below is that follow-up's starting point. The spec card and its gate decide the wording; the owner decides only if it bears on an owner point.

- Old: "The rest of the slot, or of the replica block, is zero. Card A-2 pins the bytes and fuzzes the decoder; a malformed copy is invalid (below), never a panic."
- New: "The rest of the slot, or of the replica block, is zero. Card A-2-0 pins the bytes and fuzzes the decoder. A record (page 0 or a meta copy) is **intact** when its magic and CRC are valid, and **well-formed** when it is intact and every field is in the domain card A-2-0 pins (`format_version`, `page_size`, `checksum_alg` and `wal_seg_size`; in a meta also `copy`, `map_level` and `epoch`). Up to a CRC collision, no lost, torn, misdirected or unreadable write leaves an intact record that is not well-formed: one is written whole by another format version, a faulty writer or on purpose, so the open never reads it as torn (below).
