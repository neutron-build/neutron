## Must fix (S-2-F1)

1. **SSI edge lost for released large txns (§4 step 2, §8.1 step 3).** Both say that only a `Committed(c > S)` provisional owner gives `R → T`, and §4 says "Otherwise ignore it". Counter-example: SER `R` takes `S`. Large `T` commits at `c > S`, flips and releases, and its finalize is parked. `R` then reads `k`. At `L`, `T` has ended, so there is no lock-table edge. At `T_R`, the head is `Released(c > S)` and is ignored, so the edge is lost. §8.3's own "released" case relies on "the provisional entry's owner", and §2.4 says the entry *is* a version at `c`, so the text contradicts itself. Fix: "`Committed(c)` or `Released(c)` with `c > S`" in both places.
2. **`ROLLBACK TO` order breaks I-PROV-LOCK (§5.5).** The bullets remove or downgrade the lock entry *before* the provisional entry is deleted or rewritten. In that window, `k`'s entry is free while an `Open` provisional entry sits at the head. A large `T2` that acquires `k` must then settle an `Open` entry, which §2.4 forbids (and an implementation may poison). Fix: re-materialise first (provisional entry plus superseding chunk op), then recompute or remove the entry. Add mutant ROLLBACK-UNLOCK-FIRST to `t_prov_rollback_supersede` or `t_prov_inline_settle`.
3. **S-STORE is stale after the co-edit** (all three sections are in this card's Limits):
   - §10.5's Bound still argues "`visible_ts` at the flip is `T`". It must now argue `w ≤ W ≤ M` at use, `M` monotonic, and `T = M`.
   - §17 "Watermark" still names `W ≤ visible_ts` as the precondition; it must be `W ≤ M`.
   - §17 "Commit-visibility" still offers "or a session floor", which v8 §3.4 rejects.
4. **The settle stripe is called "a leaf in the lock order" (§2.4)**, yet it is taken *before* page latches, so it is not a leaf. State the lock order: the stripe goes above page latches and below lock-table entries and txn waits. Also state that the live-txn registry is a leaf, because it is read under a page latch.

## Should fix (S-2-F1)

5. **`t_w6_shape_hol` (§11.4).**
   - The 2× unrelated-commit bound is measured only "while the big txn finalizes". Extend it to the whole big txn: the materialise phase can stall small commits' group sync behind GBs of `Chunk` bytes in the one WAL queue, which is a head-of-line path §3.2 does not bound.
   - The 10 ms conflicting-resume clock starts "at the flip". For `on`, waiters resume only after the marker's sync, which also syncs step 1′'s up-to-`ws_mem` (64 MiB) of chunks. Either sync step 1′'s chunks before step 1, or measure from marker durability.
6. **Fast path (§4).** Say that the per-tree count is read after `S` is registered (for SER, after step 2). Name a mutant, e.g. COUNT-DEC-EARLY or COUNT-BEFORE-SNAPSHOT, and a test.
7. **Name catching tests for VISIBLE-BEFORE-STATE and LOOKUP-WAITS.**
8. **The boot checkpoint (§2.5, §3.7, S-STORE §14 step 8)** must:
   - be *durable* (meta synced) before the first txn;
   - always write a new meta, even when nothing is dirty;

   Also rename "boot epoch" (for example to boot id), because it collides with S-STORE's checkpoint epoch `e`, and it is off by one from it.
9. **AS OF samples are in memory (§9.1).** After every open, the window term is 0 until a sample ages past the cutoff, so `W` (and therefore GC) freezes for one window length per open. Embedded and browser apps that reopen often may never GC. Either persist the samples in the meta, or drop the term when the window is 0 and record the pause.

## Rulings parked for the gate

1. **Owner lookup under the page latch: accepted.** The live-txn registry is in memory, holds only current-epoch txns that have not finalized, is never persisted or truncated against readers, and is never consulted for a dead entry. Deregistration follows the last deletion under an exclusive latch. No reader triggers work: finalize is the owner's own continuation, and settlers (b) and (c) act only on their own key or on dead entries. That is N2's "no resolver, no status table" in substance. The condition is item 4: the registry must be a leaf lock.
2. **Large-txn defaults: reasonable as placeholders, since R-1 owns the numbers.**
   - 4,096 rows / 4 MiB fits `wal_seg_size/4` (16 MiB) and bounds a small apply at a few ms in memory.
   - B-1d should also measure the stall behind a 4,095-row commit.
   - 10 ms p99 and 2× are acceptable once item 5 is fixed.
3. **W6 O(keys) release: the reading is accepted.** Conflict outcome and wakeups become O(1) + O(waiters), and the O(keys) purge only reclaims memory and changes no outcome. RELEASE-OKEYS with `t_release_o1_counter` pins it. The purge stays charged to `lock_mem` until it finishes.
4. **SSI (R4): the argument is sound**, given item 1 and with `F` made a store under the partition mutex (or release/acquire paired with `L`). I checked every case: B<A; A<B with `W` live (Committed counts as live); released with an ordinary version, or with a provisional one (after item 1); a conversion between `L` and `T_R`; rolled-back data; runs; phantoms; GC (`w ≤ W ≤ S < c`). CHECK-BEFORE-FLAG is a real counter-schedule. Replacing the O(write set) re-check under the SSI mutex is right.
5. **Escalation divergence: documented for NOWAIT/SKIP LOCKED, but under-stated.**
   - §12 and O4 must say plainly that *ordinary* writers to untouched keys and gaps inside the hull block, which adds 40P01 exposure.
   - With random keys (UUID), the hull is about the whole tree, which is about a relation lock.
   - O4's trigger should include blocked writers, not only queue starvation.
   - This goes to S-2a.
6. **Boot checkpoint at every open: the cost is acceptable.** A clean open writes about one meta plus a sync. After a crash it writes the replayed pages, which PostgreSQL's end-of-recovery checkpoint also does. The conditions are item 8.
7. **Unbounded long-reader disk growth in 2.0: confirmed** (PostgreSQL 17 parity, with a revisit trigger). **Flag for the owner:** in embedded and browser mode (OPFS quotas, no DBA), disk exhaustion is 53100, which poisons the store (S-STORE §9.2), and the boot checkpoint then needs free space to reopen. The owner should accept this, or ask for embedded defaults for `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` / `transaction_timeout`.
