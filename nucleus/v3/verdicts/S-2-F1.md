## Rulings on the author's choices

- **`Released(⊥)`: accepted.** A no-ts commit has to be *ended*, so its entries are free, and *committed*, so SSI's read-only exception applies to it as T1. A ts sentinel would wrongly pass §4's `c ≤ S` test, and `⊥` forces each site to handle it explicitly. The state is unreachable at a provisional head, because `ROLLBACK TO` step 2 deleted every provisional entry. Two non-blocking gaps:
  - the §2.4 settle table and the §4 head rule do not list `⊥` (F2-c);
  - v7.8 §8 decides "commits first" by `commit_ts`, and a `⊥` T2/T3 has none. Its in-edges come only from rolled-back placements, so any choice is sound and this is precision only. S-2b should state the rule: `⊥` never commits first.
- **`chunk_batch` = 1 MiB, number owned by R-1: accepted as a placeholder.** But one `Chunk` record may be up to `wal_seg_size/4`, which is above 1 MiB. The rule must therefore read "at most `chunk_batch` bytes **or one record**", or R-1 must cap chunk records at `chunk_batch` (F2-b). B-1d should also measure the big txn's own cost of one sync per batch, including on OPFS.
- **Renamed invariant IDs: accepted.**
  - I-EPOCH → I-BOOT-ID, EPOCH-REUSE → BOOT-ID-REUSE, `t_epoch_reuse_crash` → `t_boot_id_reuse_crash`.
  - The changelog records the mapping, and no code, script or other doc used the old names. The 8.0 changelog line and `verdicts/S-2.md` stay as history.
  - I-PROV-FASTPATH, I-LOCK-ORDER and I-HOL are well formed.
